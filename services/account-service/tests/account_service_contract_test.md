# Account Service Contract

## Required Endpoints

- `POST /v1/accounts/register`
- `POST /v1/accounts/login`
- `POST /v1/accounts/logout`
- `GET /v1/accounts/me`
- `GET /v1/providers`
- `GET /healthz`

## Notes

- Local account flows are active in V1.
- Social login providers are interface placeholders in V1.
